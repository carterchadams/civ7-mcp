# Treasury, resource allocation and economic reserves

**Use when:** Use when spending scarce Gold or Influence, assigning resources, responding to a deficit, or comparing immediate investment with a cash reserve.

Needs to read: context, decisions, economy, military, resources, rules, trade. Needs to execute (only if enabled): production.purchase, resources.assign, units.upgrade. Rules: R-GOVERNMENT, R-TRADE, R-VICTORY.

## Required situation

Read Gold and Influence stocks and net flows, maintenance commitments, legal purchases/upgrades, owned and imported resource inventory, slot eligibility, active assignments and disclosed connection dependencies. An inventory count is not the same as the number of copies currently providing an effect.

## Budget procedure

1. Identify unavoidable or time-critical spending over a short visible horizon. Reserve enough for those specific needs rather than using one fixed Gold threshold for every Age and game speed.
2. Classify surplus funds: emergency buffer, funded strategic commitment, and discretionary investment. Avoid both spending everything because a button is enabled and hoarding indefinitely without a purpose.
3. Assess deficits as a time-to-problem calculation using the actual rules and current flow. Do not assume a particular bankruptcy penalty. Ask which maintenance source, lost route or temporary effect explains the change.
4. Prefer expenditures that remove a current constraint or advance a realistic finish. Compare against retaining cash for a pending upgrade, conversion, reinforcement or diplomatic response.
5. After spending or allocating resources, read back the affected stocks, net flows and dependent choices. Do not keep using affordability estimates from before a purchase.

## Resource procedure

Read each resource’s current class, effect, scope, stacking limits, eligible recipients and actual assignment requirements. Do not assume a Civ VI strategic-resource stockpile or luxury-amenity model. Re-evaluate on an Age change or trade disruption.

Assign percentage-style bonuses where their marginal gain is greatest, unless another city needs the resource to clear a critical constraint. Flat bonuses can be stronger in a weak but strategically essential settlement. Compare all legal recipients, not just the place where the resource originated. Replacing a previous assignment must include the opportunity cost at its old recipient.

Bundle related changes when the host offers a validated allocation transaction. When it does not, execute a safe sequence and re-evaluate after each confirmed change; never transiently overassign a limited supply. Verify that the desired bonus actually became active.

## Failure and degraded mode

A terrain resource icon does not establish extraction, network availability or an active bonus. If slotting/connection data is missing, report the gap rather than recommending a City conversion merely because a deposit is in a Town. Resource information omitted by the observer is not proof the empire owns none.
