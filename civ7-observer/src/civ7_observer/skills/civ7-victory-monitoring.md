# Victory monitoring, commitment and denial

**Use when:** Use every turn to inspect enabled finish conditions, detect imminent rival wins and choose whether to continue, pivot or interrupt.

Needs to read: context, diplomacy, economy, memory, military, rules, victory. Rules: R-VICTORY.

## Required situation

Read each enabled track’s current score, disclosed comparison benchmark, eligibility, required threshold, countdown state, relevant infrastructure conditions and public rival warnings. Include missing/hidden flags. A scoreboard visible to a normal player can be exposed; privileged rival production queues cannot.

## Procedure

1. Inspect actual finish conditions before broad development. Identify an active own or rival countdown, approaching threshold changes and prerequisites whose loss would affect a finish.
2. Maintain one primary finish and, when useful, one fallback. Compare plausible completion time, setup expense, vulnerability and actual opportunity, not just the largest current yield or score.
3. Distinguish a lead from sufficient dominance. For a ratio-based track, inspect both the numerator and comparison benchmark. Increasing your score can still leave you farther from eligibility when the benchmark grows faster.
4. If a rival can finish sooner, consider three responses: finish first, legally interrupt a specific qualifying condition, or improve the relevant benchmark enough to change eligibility. Choose only operations that can matter before the deadline. An unrelated war is not denial.
5. When protecting your own countdown, prioritize continuity of its actual conditions. A speculative additional score gain may be inferior to defending the one exposed asset whose loss would interrupt the finish.
6. Recheck after a material scoring event, conquest, delivery, work assignment, infrastructure loss or threshold change. Do not defer monitoring for dozens of turns because no city needs a production choice.
7. Confirm the game’s win/loss result. Do not announce victory based solely on a forecast or the intention to end a turn.

## Forecasting without cheating

Use disclosed rates and events to make a bounded scenario forecast. Separate observed scoreboard data from estimates of future rival behavior. Report a range when event timing or opponent growth is uncertain. Never query a hidden queue or unrevealed city to improve the forecast.

The host should surface finish alerts in the default turn envelope so the agent cannot forget to request them. This is a proposed monitoring design; the skill still must attend to the alert and act. A reminder is not an automatic competent response.
