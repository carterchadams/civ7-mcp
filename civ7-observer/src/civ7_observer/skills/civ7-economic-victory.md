# Economic victory execution

**Use when:** Use when evaluating or pursuing Economic victory to build an actual GDP-scoring system rather than merely accumulating a large treasury.

Needs to read: context, economy, map, military, production, resources, rules, trade, victory. Needs to execute (only if enabled): production.choose, production.purchase, resources.assign, settlements.convert, trade.deliver, trade.establish. Rules: R-TOWN, R-TRADE, R-VICTORY.

## Required situation

Read GDP and its player-visible contributions, eligible resource and building effects, active trade/settlement connections, cargo generation and delivery state, factory-related eligibility where relevant, and the disclosed comparative threshold. Gold balance, Gold flow and GDP are separate measures.

## Procedure

1. Identify the current scoring bottleneck. Is the empire missing eligible assignments, productive scoring infrastructure, a connection, a delivery, a prerequisite, or protection for an existing system?
2. Prefer completing a nearly functional chain before starting several disconnected schemes. For an overseas plan, account for the entire source-to-delivery path; for a domestic plan, ensure the relevant settlement and assignment actually qualify.
3. Compare recurring scoring flow with one-time rewards over the realistic finish horizon. Include opportunity cost, setup delay and probability of successful delivery. A theoretical stream that begins after the rival finishes has little defensive value.
4. Improve resource use across eligible recipients. Check actual category/age rules and avoid counting unassigned, disconnected or otherwise inactive resources as already producing their intended benefit.
5. Fund economic infrastructure without eliminating the defense of routes and productive settlements. Escorts, reinforcements and diplomacy can be part of the economic investment rather than competing themes.
6. Monitor inventories, route expirations, cargo readiness, prerequisites and receipts. An automatic cargo spawn is not the same as a delivered reward. A failed or interrupted route must update the scoring forecast.
7. Compare GDP growth to the benchmark and actual finish condition after each significant event. Spend saved Gold when that accelerates a credible finish; do not hoard it merely to look rich on the report.

## City and industry decisions

When considering conversion or a factory chain, read the live qualifying conditions. Do not assume a Gold-generating Town counts as every type of scoring City infrastructure, or that a factory building alone activates all factory-resource benefits. The useful choice may be a resource reassignment, a connection or one missing prerequisite rather than another settlement.

Avoid rote treasure instructions from an old victory checklist. A cargo operation should be undertaken because its current score/economic return beats the alternatives, not because every economic game is assumed to require the same number of deliveries.
