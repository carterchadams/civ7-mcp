# Age transition, civilization choice and crisis response
<!-- Runtime-maintained: edit this card directly; build_skills.py preserves it. -->

**Use when:** Use as an Age nears its end, during crisis choices, at civilization/Dedication selection, and immediately after entering a new Age.

Needs to read: context, decisions, economy, military, rules, trade, transition, triumphs, victory. Needs to execute (only if enabled): crisis.choose, dedications.choose, government.choose, transition.choose. Rules: R-CIV, R-GOVERNMENT, R-TRANSITION, R-TRIUMPH.

## Required situation

Read disclosed Age progress, transition mode/options, civilization variants, active crisis and mandatory policy/event choices, surviving/persistent effects, current commitments and near-term victory conditions. Obtain an actual transition preview when supported; otherwise mark uncertain persistence rather than presenting an old guide’s reset list as guaranteed.

## Before transition

Review unfinished investments for current-Age payoff, persistence and opportunity cost. Keep funds for credible incoming rebuilding/reorganization needs without neglecting present defense. Finish worthwhile nearby objectives; do not panic-buy every incomplete Triumph. Inspect any imminent victory window before assuming the Age boundary is a harmless reset.

Compare continuing the civilization with legal alternatives. Evaluate the concrete next-Age ability package against the empire’s terrain, settlement roles, surviving infrastructure, military posture and intended finish. Do not pick a famous “best civ” without a usable setup. Include Syncretism/Affirmation decisions where actually available; select the effect that solves a problem or strengthens an affordable plan.

## During crisis

Identify the current penalties, required choices and affected settlements. Compare mitigation by its marginal damage reduction and cost. Stabilize supply, defense and critical output before chasing an opportunistic reward. A temporary harmful policy may be mandatory; choose the least damaging option for the actual empire, not the least alarming name.

Avoid assuming the same crisis appears in every game or that an announced future mode is already implemented. Use the live crisis definition and choices. Treat rare narrative, attribute or dedication screens as real decisions with explicit outcomes, not UI popups to dismiss blindly.

## After transition

1. Refresh session/context and rules/ability fingerprints; remap changed entities through stable host identity rules.
2. Rebuild the role map for settlements and forces. Inspect Town/City status, available queues, maintenance, unit readiness and new research/civic options.
3. Re-evaluate connection and resource assignments, Great Work slots, diplomacy and surviving effects. Read actual state rather than assuming everything either persisted or vanished.
4. Resolve government, policy, Dedication and other mandatory setup choices in native dependency order.
5. Select a small next-Age plan with funded first steps. Clear obsolete pending action IDs and cancel commitments whose prerequisites no longer exist.

## Exploration Age opening review

After resolving transition setup, choose the next plan from the refreshed map and rules, not the previous Age's queue. Revisit known Independent sites and relations: do not assume allies, hostility, projects or bonuses persisted. Assign Influence to the best newly available partnership, access or defense opportunity, with a named response reserve.

Renew exploration immediately: new travel access, resources and objectives can make a previously irrelevant coast, island or land corridor valuable. Pair an expedition commitment with homeland defense and a funded economy. Use `civ7-naval-and-overseas` for the route/foothold/delivery checks even if the best route is over land.

Check actual enabled Age objectives and their bottlenecks: eligible Distant Lands holdings and Treasure deliveries where relevant, religious choices and relic opportunities, specialist/science capacity, military reach and diplomatic access. Do not attempt every track at once. Choose a primary payoff and a compatible secondary opportunity; confirm native scoring and prerequisites before redirecting investment. Update resource assignments, settlement roles, government/policies and production for that choice.

Reassess when a route opens, an Independent becomes hostile/friendly, the first delivery succeeds, or the Age horizon shortens. Cancel expeditions and diplomatic contests that can no longer repay their remaining cost. Clear old action handles and retain only current-generation commitments with verified prerequisites.
