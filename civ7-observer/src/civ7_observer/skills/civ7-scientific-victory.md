# Scientific victory execution

**Use when:** Use when evaluating or pursuing Scientific victory to convert research and production into the actual Innovation and infrastructure requirements.

Needs to read: context, economy, great_works, military, production, research, rules, victory. Needs to execute (only if enabled): great_works.assign, production.choose, production.purchase, research.choose. Rules: R-PREVIEW, R-VICTORY.

## Required situation

Read Scientific victory eligibility, current Innovation, sources available in this Age, project prerequisites and rewards, relevant technology/mastery choices, eligible Codex slots, the state of required infrastructure, and threats to productive centers. Use actual descriptions rather than a remembered sequence of named space projects.

## Procedure

1. Separate the research engine from the finish. Science output opens options; the win requires its own reported conditions. Identify the remaining score and prerequisite gap explicitly.
2. Construct a dependency plan ending at the finish: research/mastery prerequisites, required infrastructure, legal projects or other scoring actions, funding and completion times. Assign each production step to a City that can actually perform it.
3. Compare sources by marginal progress per constrained resource and by time to completion. A modest available scoring action can be better than a distant large unlock, while a new research investment can be worthwhile when it unlocks multiple efficient steps.
4. Use existing eligible assets before constructing replacements. Verify that a Codex or other qualifying item is active in a valid slot and that its contribution is reflected by the scoreboard.
5. Concentrate necessary production without bankrupting defense or Food/Happiness support. When parallelization is legal, use it; when a project is restricted, do not invent a second copy or a bypass.
6. Recompute the gap after each confirmed scoring event. Avoid continuing an obsolete project plan that overshoots the needed score while the real remaining bottleneck is infrastructure or protection.
7. Once eligible, defend the qualifying condition and monitor the actual timer. Respond to damage, loss or interruption using the game’s rule, not an assumption that reaching the score permanently guarantees victory.

## Development emphasis

A healthy science strategy still needs Culture, production capacity, treasury flexibility, map information and security. Do not fill every urban slot merely because the label “Specialist” sounds scientific. Use the growth and placement skills to create a sustainable engine, then consciously switch from broad development to completion when further research no longer shortens the finish.

If another victory is substantially closer with the current empire, compare the pivot rather than remaining attached to a science-themed leader. Preserve the scientific fallback only while it has a credible use.
