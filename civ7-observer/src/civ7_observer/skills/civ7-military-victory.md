# Military victory and sustainable territorial dominance

**Use when:** Use when evaluating or pursuing Military victory to select territorial objectives that change Dominion while remaining defensible and economically sustainable.

Needs to read: commanders, context, diplomacy, economy, map, military, rules, trade, victory. Needs to execute (only if enabled): diplomacy.propose, settlements.capture_decision, settlements.found, units.attack, units.move. Rules: R-COMBAT, R-COMMAND, R-DIPLOMACY, R-VICTORY.

## Required situation

Read Dominion and its current contribution rules, disclosed competitor benchmark, relevant known settlements, available peaceful expansion/integration options, ready force and capture capabilities, empire stability and diplomatic dependencies. Do not equate military strength with the victory score or assume every territorial gain has identical value.

## Procedure

1. Estimate which territorial change actually advances the finish. Compare a safe founding, a legal peaceful integration, a valuable capture or consolidation of existing territory using the current scoring rules.
2. For conquest, select a bounded target with a useful score or strategic effect. Include travel, defenses, required capture actions, likely visible opposition and post-capture holding cost. Do not choose only by proximity or prestige.
3. Check the whole-empire consequence: settlement headroom, Happiness, maintenance, damaged infrastructure, diplomatic response and supply. A capture that collapses the rest of the empire can lengthen rather than shorten the route to victory.
4. Coordinate the force-planning, Commander and combat skills. Start only when the relevant operation is ready; the victory objective does not waive ordinary logistics or legal capture requirements.
5. After a territorial change, read the actual score and benchmark. Do not infer automatic point theft from the defeated opponent or assume a settlement’s classification from its apparent location.
6. Prefer holding and stabilizing enough territory over endless conquest when that satisfies the finish. Defend the marginal vulnerable settlement whose loss would interrupt a countdown.
7. Evaluate peace after each completed objective. A favorable agreement can preserve dominance while freeing the economy. If the objective has become unattainable before another player finishes, pivot or seek a specific timely denial rather than continuing a sunk-cost campaign.

## Balanced expansion

Military victory does not mean declaring war on every known leader. Some territorial opportunity may be peaceful and some wars contribute little to the actual score. Concentrate effort where the legal scoring and operational cost align. Human opponents may counterattack a lightly held gain, so a capture plan must include a garrison or relief plan rather than immediately moving everything onward.

Denial and conquest are different operations. Damaging a rival’s economy may slow future growth without interrupting the active victory condition in time. Choose the exact asset or threshold interaction that matters, and label uncertainty when only part of the rival’s situation is disclosed.
