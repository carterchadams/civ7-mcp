# Cultural victory execution

**Use when:** Use when evaluating or pursuing Cultural victory to grow the actual Tourism score efficiently while preserving its scoring assets and finish conditions.

Needs to read: context, economy, great_works, map, production, religion, rules, victory. Needs to execute (only if enabled): celebration.choose, great_works.assign, production.choose, production.purchase, religion.choose. Rules: R-GOVERNMENT, R-PREVIEW, R-RELIGION, R-VICTORY.

## Required situation

Read the current cultural score, disclosed benchmark, scoring breakdown, available scoring investments, eligible works/slots, relevant civilization infrastructure, celebration effects and the actual age-dependent threshold. Culture yield and cultural victory progress must be represented separately.

## Procedure

1. Identify the engine the current empire can support: existing productive centers and suitable placements, usable special infrastructure, Great Work opportunities, a strong celebration economy or another explicitly scored effect. Do not assume every culture-themed action increases the victory score.
2. Compare complete investment packages. Include prerequisite research, production, placement opportunity cost, acquisition/delivery and activation requirements. If a work needs a slot, price the slot as well as the work.
3. Evaluate Wonders individually. Their score contribution can be attractive, but a long contested project may sacrifice multiple smaller reliable gains or essential defense. Use only public race information; do not peek at rival queues.
4. Preserve and use existing scoring assets. Check assignment, active effects and vulnerability before building new ones. Do not assume captured and self-built assets score identically; read the actual breakdown.
5. Use Happiness/government decisions with the victory context in mind, without ignoring their economic cost. A temporary gain must arrive during a relevant window to justify redirecting the empire.
6. Track the moving benchmark. A steady cultural lead can still be insufficient; choose the action that closes the qualifying gap fastest, not merely the one with the most attractive cultural flavor.
7. Once a finish is within reach, defend the assets and conditions that maintain it. Avoid sacrificing a stable countdown for an unnecessary conquest or another speculative Wonder.

## Sustainable cultural play

Production and treasury flexibility are often the means by which a culture-oriented plan becomes tangible. Keep enough research and diplomacy to protect that investment. A large Culture-per-turn number that only unlocks more optional choices is not proof the actual finish is progressing.

Do not treat religion as compulsory when available secular or civilization-specific scoring opportunities offer a cheaper path. Conversely, ignore neither beliefs nor works when an already-built system can generate relevant gains with little extra cost.
