## Reading and decision

- Per seat turn, within the Exploration age where all three periods have data, Stage 1 at medium effort is faster than the pre-Stage-1 Exploration turns (median 80.6 s against 100.6 s) while performing about twice the receipts per turn (18.8 against 15.9, on larger empires); at low effort the median falls further (64.4 s) with more receipts still (23.8). Throughput rose from 7.2 to 8.9 to 10.8 complete rounds per wall-clock hour, failures and restarts included.
- The earlier plan text compared a 24-turn window (59.3 s) with a 109.3 s mean; that comparison mixed ages and is superseded by the tables above.
- Quality signals do not show a systematic regression attributable to the prompt: distinct action kinds per turn are level (5.0 / 5.5 / 5.0), pauses and friction fell, and the drop in turns with a move or explore in the low-effort period reflects scouts placed on automate_explore and standing orders (a directive of 2026-09-17), not units left idle. Unknown outcomes rose in the medium-effort period (20 in 212 turns against 11 in 505); every one traced to a harness read-back gap that was fixed in the same period (wake, stopped-short moves, town focus, great-work identity), and the low-effort period has none so far.
- Cost per turn roughly doubled from the pre-Stage-1 period, driven by the action count (about 2x receipts and tool calls), not by the prompt; the dollar figures are the client's estimate, not subscription charges.
- **Decision: keep Stage 1** (replacement prompt, effort flag, bounded opportunity check). The medium/low effort choice is an operator setting; low effort is in use for the testing-focused run at the operator's request and has not shown a quality regression in 22 turns, which is too few to close that question. Optional prompt profiles and receipt follow-up options remain unstarted and outside this report.
